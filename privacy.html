Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to MK Studio. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you may share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website or download the MK Studio Android application.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

MK Studio is designed with a strict privacy-first architecture:

  • We do NOT require user account creation or registration.
  • We do NOT track your precise GPS location or contacts.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, or trade any user data to third-party advertisers.

3. App Permissions

The MK Studio APK requests only the minimum runtime permissions required for its core functionality:

  • Storage / Media: Required solely for local file processing or caching user preferences.
  • Internet Access: Used for checking GitHub Release updates and downloading official patch packages.

4. Security & Malware Protection

All APK release files distributed via MK Studio undergo automated VirusTotal scans and SHA-256 checksum validation prior to publishing. We guarantee 100% clean, virus-free builds with zero spyware or hidden tracking routines.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact Page or email support at support@mkstudio.app.
